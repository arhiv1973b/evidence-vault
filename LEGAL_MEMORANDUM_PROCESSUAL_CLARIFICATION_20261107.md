# LEGAL MEMORANDUM AND PROCEDURAL CLARIFICATION

**Case Reference:** CASE-MACHERET-1997-2026  
**Subject:** Clarification of compliance with judicial fee obligations (stamp duty / state fee) and elimination of any alleged inconsistency.

**To:** Chișinău Court (Centru seat, Judge Iraida Secrieru-Coșleț), MONEYVAL Secretariat, international AML compliance structures.

---

## PREAMBLE AND LEGAL STATUS

This clarification is submitted within the civil case initiated by Macheret Alexei (A©tor), IDNP: 2000001159655, against B.C. „FinComBank” S.A. and „Moldcell” S.A., in execution of the ruling of Judge Iraida Secrieru-Coșleț dated 04.11.2026 (case No. 2-20128/26), concerning the elimination of deficiencies.

This document officially consolidates the continuity and consistency of the claimant’s legal position and rejects any artificial accusations of “inconsistency” regarding the payment of stamp duty and state fee.

---

## I. JUSTIFICATION OF THE CONSISTENCY OF PROCEDURAL ACTIONS AND ELIMINATION OF THE ALLEGED “INCONSISTENCY”

### 1. Principle of individual assessment of jurisdictional disputes and procedural evolution

Previous objections and judicial disputes concerning stamp duty (including the successful challenge of certain rulings, particularly in the case involving Judge Lupașcu of the Court of Appeal) concerned other objects of substantive legal disputes (in particular, disputes regarding apostille and administrative refusals), in which the application of stamp duty was contested due to the special nature of the legal relationships and the direct application of imperative guarantees of social protection of a person with severe disability, classified in Group I disability.

### 2. Priority of access to justice over formal obstacles (Exhaustion and Compliance)

In the present judicial procedure, the object of which is the protection of consumer rights, the establishment of the illegality of the actions of the telecommunications operator and the credit institution, as well as the unblocking of assets in the amount of 25,210,256.15 MDL, the claimant faces unprecedented institutional sabotage and obstruction of access to justice.

In order to exclude any procedural pretext that the court or the defendants might invoke with a view to delaying the proceedings (Procedural Denial of Justice), the claimant exercised the right to voluntarily comply with the court’s requirements regarding the payment of fees, attaching to the statement of claim of 06.11.2026 the original payment documents (Moldindconbank receipt in the amount of 200 MDL).

### 3. Absence of any contradiction between the positions expressed

- **Position 1 (of principle):** The requirements regarding the payment of stamp duty in disputes affecting fundamental constitutional rights and the social rights of a person with Group I disability were contested as representing a financial burden limiting access to the court.
- **Position 2 (procedural and practical):** The payment of the fee in the current dispute, the object of which is the sum of 25 million lei, was made exclusively in execution of the court’s intermediate indications, for the purpose of eliminating formal obstacles (deficiencies), which fully corresponds to the principle of procedural estoppel and the exercise of rights in good faith.

Payment of the fee under the threat of return of the statement of claim does not constitute a waiver of substantive claims regarding the illegal nature of the fiscal pressure, but represents a measure intended to eliminate artificial formal grounds for refusal to examine the case on the merits.

---

## II. TAKING INTO ACCOUNT THE SOCIAL STATUS AND THE NATURE OF THE DISPUTE

- The claimant is a person with Group I disability, which, on the basis of the fundamental principles of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948) and imperative norms (jus cogens, erga omnes), imposes on the State an enhanced positive obligation to ensure effective and unimpeded access to means of judicial protection, and not to establish barriers in the form of judicial fees.
- The fact of payment of the stamp duty on 06.11.2026 constitutes a procedural step imposed by circumstances, aimed at overcoming the procedural obstacles created by the judicial system (ruling of Judge I. Secrieru-Coșleț of 04.11.2026) and at ensuring the prompt examination of the case concerning the diversion and blocking of the sum of 25.2 million MDL.

---

## FINAL REQUEST

On the basis of the foregoing, any attempt to qualify the claimant’s actions regarding the payment of the stamp duty as an “inconsistent position” is unfounded, manipulative and aimed at concealing the material elements of the alleged violations attributed to B.C. „FinComBank” S.A. and „Moldcell” S.A.

The court is obliged to accept the claim for examination on the merits, since all formal requirements, including those concerning the attachment of receipts for the payment of judicial fees, have been fully complied with.
